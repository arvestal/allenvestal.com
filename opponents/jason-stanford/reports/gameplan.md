# Defensive game plan vs. Jason (Stanford) — 3-3-5 Tite

## What the data says

- **He's averaging 10.3 yards per play with a 70% success rate.** He moves the ball on almost every down.
- **1st & 10 is where he hurts you:** 94 snaps, 12.2 yds/play, 35% gain 15+. Most of his explosive plays come on 1st down.
- **2nd & long is where he struggles:** 6.6 yds/play, 44% success. **Win 1st down and he gets much worse.**
- **Cover 3 is the worst call against him:** 16.5 yds allowed per play, 41% explosive. On 1st down it's 19.8 yds per play and 60% explosive.
  Snag beat Cover 3 Cloud for 41, Double Slant beat Buzz Match for 28, HB Slip Screen beat Match for 27,
  Curls Slot Shake beat Hard Flat for 23, and Triple Slant beat Match for 21.
- **Cover 4 isn't working either:** 12.3 yds per play, 77% success for him, and 17.6 on 1st down.
- **Two-deep and man coverage hold up best:** Cover 2 Man allows 7.6 yds per play (18% explosive), Double/Bracket 8.1 (14%),
  Cover 2 Zone/Invert 4.5, and Cover 6/9 3.0.
- **Blitzing doesn't work:** 83% success for him, 11.3 yds per play. He throws before the pressure arrives.
- **Red zone:** 12 TDs on 25 snaps. Cover 6/9 gave up 4 TDs on 6 goal-to-go snaps.

## Pre-snap adjustments (from your Coverage Adjustments menu)

- **Press** on the outside WRs on every early down.
- **Inside** shade in man against his slant and Snag formations (Normal Y Off Close, Trips Y Slot Wk).
- **Sticks** on 3rd and medium or long: zone defenders drop to the first-down marker.
- **Show 2** before the snap, then rotate after the snap. He reads one-high looks.
